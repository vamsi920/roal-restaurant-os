**First comment (maker):**

Hey Product Hunt,

I'm Vamsi, and I build ROAL from Jersey City. Small restaurants get most of their phone calls exactly when nobody can pick up: Friday at 7pm, with a full counter and a backed-up kitchen. Those calls are orders that go to the place down the street.

ROAL is an AI phone host for restaurants:

1. **Paste your link.** Your website or Google Maps listing. ROAL reads your menu, prices and hours.
2. **Call it.** Test your own host in the browser in about two minutes. Order something, change your mind, ask when you close.
3. **Forward your number.** Orders land on your kitchen screen with items, modifiers, name, phone and ready time.

It speaks the caller's language, skips sold-out items, and hands catering, complaints and allergy questions to a person with a summary. It never takes card numbers over the phone.

**Pricing is one number: $0.90 per completed order.** No monthly fee, no setup fee, no per-minute charges, no contract. If a call doesn't become an order, it's free.

If you know a restaurant owner, I'd love for you to send them the link. And I'd love your honest feedback: what would make you trust a phone host with your Friday rush?
